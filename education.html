Education

Do I have to take a homebuyer class?

Don’t worry — homebuyer education is designed to help you understand the process, not make buying a home harder.

Many SONYMA programs require borrowers to complete an approved homebuyer education course before closing. The exact requirement depends on the specific program and your situation. Your lender will confirm what applies to your loan.

What the class typically covers

Understanding credit

How credit works, what lenders look at, and steps you can take to improve or maintain a strong profile.

Your mortgage

How mortgages work, interest, principal, payment structures, and key terms you will see on loan documents.

Budgeting & costs

Planning for the full cost of homeownership beyond the mortgage payment — taxes, insurance, maintenance, and reserves.

Avoiding common mistakes

Practical guidance on contracts, inspections, timing, and decisions that can protect you during the purchase process.

When a certificate may be required

If your SONYMA program requires education, you will generally need to complete an approved course and provide a certificate of completion before closing. Online options are often available. The specific course and any time limits are confirmed by your lender based on current SONYMA guidelines.

Do not assume every SONYMA borrower takes the exact same course. Requirements can differ by program and borrower profile. Always confirm with your loan officer.

Why it exists

Homebuyer education is part of SONYMA’s broader goal of supporting successful, sustainable homeownership. The intent is to give buyers clear, practical information so they can make informed decisions and avoid common pitfalls.
