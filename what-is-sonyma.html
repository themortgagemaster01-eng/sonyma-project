SONYMA, explained

What is the State of New York Mortgage Agency — and why does it exist?

SONYMA stands for the State of New York Mortgage Agency. It is a New York State public benefit corporation created to help make mortgage financing more available to qualifying homebuyers.

In practical terms: SONYMA partners with participating lenders (like Movement Mortgage) to offer program options that may include competitive fixed rates, down payment assistance, and features designed for eligible New York buyers.

How SONYMA differs from a typical conventional loan

Program structure

SONYMA programs have specific income limits, purchase-price limits, and property rules that vary by county and Target Area status.

Assistance options

Eligible borrowers may be able to use SONYMA’s Down Payment Assistance Loan (DPAL) — often 0% interest with no monthly payment, subject to guidelines.
