Basics

What SONYMA is

SONYMA (State of New York Mortgage Agency) partners with participating lenders to offer mortgage programs for eligible New York homebuyers. Features can include competitive fixed rates, lower down-payment options, and down-payment assistance (DPAL).

What is SONYMA? → · Why SONYMA? →

Borrower

First-time homebuyer rule

Most SONYMA programs require you to be a first-time homebuyer — generally meaning you have not had an ownership interest in a principal residence in the last 3 years.

  • Buying in a federally designated Target Area can relax the first-time rule (subject to program rules).
  • Some limited exceptions may apply (for example, certain veterans) under current guidelines.
  • You must occupy the home as your primary residence.
Location

Target Areas

Target Areas are designated geographic areas where SONYMA may allow:

  • More flexible first-time buyer rules
  • Higher income limits in many cases
  • Higher purchase-price limits in many cases

Look up any address: SONYMA Target Area Locator →

Limits

Income & purchase-price limits

Limits vary by program (e.g. Achieving the Dream vs Low Interest Rate), county/region, household size, and whether the property is in a Target Area.

  • Income is generally based on total household income under program definitions.
  • Purchase-price limits also vary by property type (1–4 unit).
  • Limits change periodically — treat any online tool as educational only.

Property

Eligible property types

Subject to program rules, SONYMA financing may be available for:

  • 1–4 family primary residences
  • Condominiums
  • Cooperatives (co-ops)
  • Certain manufactured homes on fee-simple land

Cash contribution rules can differ (for example, co-ops often require a higher minimum borrower contribution).

Assistance

DPAL & DPAL PLUS

DPAL (Down Payment Assistance Loan) is typically a 0% interest second loan with no monthly payment, used for down payment and eligible costs. Using standard DPAL usually increases the first-mortgage rate (often about 0.40%).

DPAL PLUS is a limited, enhanced assistance option for certain lower-income buyers, with stricter limits and funding caps. Not every borrower qualifies, and funds can run out.

Education

Homebuyer education

Many SONYMA loans require completion of an approved homebuyer education course before closing. Your lender confirms what applies to your file.

Homebuyer education overview →

Tax note

Federal recapture tax

Recapture tax does not automatically apply just because you use SONYMA. It may only come into play if several conditions are all true when you sell (timing, gain, and income thresholds). Most borrowers never owe it.

Recapture tax explained →

Rates

Rates & locks

SONYMA rates are set by program and can change. Many options are 30-year fixed with 0 points. Longer rate locks (for example, 120 days on existing homes) may be available depending on program terms.
