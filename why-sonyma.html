At a glance

Core SONYMA programs

Educational overview — not a commitment or approval.

AT
Primary program

Achieving the Dream

Designed for lower-income households. Competitive 30-year fixed pricing and the lowest rate path among the main SONYMA first-mortgage options.

  • 30-year fixed · 0 points
  • Down payment as low as 3%
  • Can pair with DPAL / DPAL PLUS
  • Income & purchase-price limits by county
From 5.700% without DPAL
LR
Primary program

Low Interest Rate

Serves a broader moderate-income range with slightly higher rates than Achieving the Dream — still structured as a 30-year fixed SONYMA first mortgage.

  • 30-year fixed · 0 points
  • Down payment as low as 3%
  • Can pair with DPAL
  • Higher income bands than ATD in many counties
From 6.100% without DPAL
DP
Assistance

Standard DPAL

Down Payment Assistance Loan paired with an eligible SONYMA first mortgage. 0% interest, no monthly payment — typically forgiven over 10 years if you remain in the home.

  • Generally the greater of $3,000 or 3% of price
  • Up to $15,000 (illustrative max under common rules)
  • Requires eligible SONYMA first mortgage
  • First-time / Target Area rules apply
0% interest · no monthly payment
+
Limited funding

DPAL PLUS

Enhanced assistance for eligible buyers. Funding is limited and administered on a first-come, first-served basis under current program rules.

  • Up to $30,000 potential (program rules apply)
  • Stricter income screening in many cases
  • Purchase-price caps may apply
  • Availability can change without notice
Limited · first-come, first-served
Shared features

What both main programs share

  • 30-year fixed-rate mortgage only
  • No points
  • Down payment as low as 3% + optional DPAL assistance
  • 120-day rate lock on existing homes (extensions often available)
  • Single-family, condo, co-op, and 2–4 family primary residences
  • No prepayment penalties
  • Can be combined with other grants and subsidies in many cases
Locks

Longer rate locks

120-day locks for existing housing. Longer 240-day locks commonly available for new construction, rehab, co-ops, or distressed sales.

Target Areas

Target Area benefits

Buying in a designated Target Area may waive the first-time buyer rule and allow higher income and purchase-price limits in many counties.
