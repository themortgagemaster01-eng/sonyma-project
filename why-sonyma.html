Why are buyers choosing SONYMA?

The right mortgage depends on your situation. SONYMA may be worth exploring if its combination of rates, assistance, and program features fits your needs.

01

Lower than average rates

SONYMA is designed to offer competitive 30-year fixed rates that can help keep monthly payments more manageable for eligible New York buyers.

02

Down payment assistance

Eligible borrowers may use SONYMA’s DPAL — typically 0% interest and no monthly payment — generally 3% of the purchase price up to $15,000 (or $3,000, whichever is higher), subject to guidelines.

03

Longer lock periods

SONYMA programs often provide longer rate lock periods, giving buyers more time and flexibility while navigating the purchase process.

04

Target Area benefits

Buying in a designated Target Area may allow a waiver of the first-time homebuyer requirement, higher income limits, and higher purchase price limits.
