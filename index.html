Why SONYMA Can Matter

SONYMA can offer eligible New York buyers financing options and assistance that may make homeownership more attainable.

01 — COMPETITIVE FINANCING

Focus on the rate

Depending on the program and borrower profile, SONYMA may offer competitive 30-year fixed-rate financing.

02 — DOWN-PAYMENT ASSISTANCE

Focus on cash to close

Eligible borrowers may be able to use SONYMA Down Payment Assistance (DPAL) to help with required cash toward the purchase.

03 — TARGET AREAS

Focus on opportunity

Certain Target Areas may provide expanded eligibility or exceptions to the standard first-time-homebuyer requirement.
