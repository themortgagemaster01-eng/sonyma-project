Designed with first-time homebuyers in mind

  • Low fixed interest rate options
  • As little as 3% down on eligible programs
  • Down payment assistance may be available
  • Eligible 1–4 family homes, condos & co-ops
  • Available throughout New York State
  • Income & purchase-price limits apply

SONYMA’s Achieving the Dream program is among its lowest-rate options for eligible buyers, with flexible entry requirements compared to many conventional paths.

Need help with your down payment?

  • SONYMA Down Payment Assistance Loan (DPAL)
  • Up to 3% of the purchase price
  • Maximum $15,000 (standard DPAL)
  • May apply to down payment & eligible closing costs
  • 0% interest · No monthly payments
  • Forgiven after 10 years if program requirements are met

Program terms, funding, and eligibility are subject to change. Ask Robert for current DPAL / DPAL PLUS details.
